Crafting a Seamless Social Commerce Experience for Aspiring Entrepreneurs

JUNIOR UI/UX DESIGNER
July 2021 to February 2022

OVERVIEW: CORDOVA, a big brand book store from Makassar trusted me to craft their wild idea about MALMORA a place where people could build their business without any cost. The expected outcome of this collaboration is a usable, intuitive, and compact UI product as a social commerce.

Problem

Following Indonesia's 2021 reselling boom, resellers faced real barriers to starting or scaling: capital constraints, manual processes, and outdated catalogs that made the business harder than it needed to be. Cordova, a Makassar-based bookstore brand, wanted a platform that removed those barriers entirely.

Process

As the sole designer on the team, I ran user research directly with active resellers, synthesized findings into thematic clusters and problem maps, benchmarked competitors, then designed complete user flows and wireframes before shipping the final high-fidelity UI for a platform connecting suppliers, resellers, and customers.

Outcome

From Capital Barrier to Zero-Capital Business

Delivered an end-to-end multi-user e-commerce platform — my first professional design contract — enabling anyone to launch a reseller store without upfront inventory investment.
