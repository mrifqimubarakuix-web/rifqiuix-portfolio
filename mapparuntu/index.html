Innovating Construction Design to Unite Developers and Clients

UI/UX DESIGNER
2024

CLIENT: MAPPARUNTU is a newly founded startup based in Makassar, Indonesia, with a brilliant idea of connecting building developers with clients. It facilitates transactions and provides detailed project monitoring for the construction of homes, cafes, restaurants, or even companies through comprehensively designed tables tailored to field requirements.

PROJECT: I was entrusted with managing the entire design process, from research to the final application design tailored to the client's needs. The biggest challenge was creating a technical app for two distinct users (building developers and clients) with differing UI designs. Delivering a complex yet user-friendly interface required extensive research, testing, and revisions.
