A Unified Product to Streamline Processes and Enhance Operational Performance

PROJECT MANAGER / UI/UX DESIGNER
July 2024 - Present

CLIENT: TOYOTA KALLA is an official TOYOTA dealer operating under KALLA GROUP, a prominent company based in South Sulawesi, Indonesia. TOYOTA KALLA offers various automotive services, including TOYOTA vehicle sales, after-sales services such as maintenance and repairs, and the provision of genuine TOYOTA spare parts.

Problem

TOYOTA KALLA's branch network operated with dynamic, inconsistent workflows across locations, with no single product capable of handling seamless input-output processes and efficient data transfer between branches. What started as a branch-level initiative was significant enough to be escalated to KALLA GROUP leadership.

Process

As Project Manager and UI/UX Designer, I collaborated closely with two developers to translate branch-level pain points into a unified system — covering everything from customer data intake to vehicle inspection, offer processing, and work completion tracking, across multiple user roles and both mobile and web platforms. The biggest challenge was designing flexibility into the system without sacrificing consistency across branches with different operational habits.

Outcome

From Fragmented Branch Workflows to One Unified System

The project exceeded its initial success metrics, delivering a measurable improvement in overall operational efficiency across the branch network.
