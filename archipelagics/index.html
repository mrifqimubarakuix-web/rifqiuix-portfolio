Crafting a Standout Landing Page for Global Tuna Export Startup

UI/UX DESIGNER
2023

CLIENT: ARCHIPELAGICS is an ambitious project aimed at showcasing the quality of fish products that will eventually be directly exported from Indonesia. The client is a newly established startup team based in the United States.

PROJECT: Working remotely with regular weekly meetings focusing primarily on the quality of content presented on the landing page. The biggest challenge of this project was finding a way to combine a creative design, concise messaging, and high-quality content that effectively captures the attention of potential customers.
