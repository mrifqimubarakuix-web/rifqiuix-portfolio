Scalable Systems and User-Friendly Solutions for the FnB Industry

PRODUCT DESIGNER
April 2022 to February 2024

OVERVIEW: LUCY TECHNOLOGY is a Makassar-based startup that develops products for Food and Beverage (FnB) entrepreneurs — simplifying their operational tasks with various digital solutions — across nearly every aspect of their business.

Problem

When I joined, Lucy Technology had no design system at all — every product was built ad-hoc, making it hard to scale consistently as the company grew across nearly every aspect of FnB operations.

Process

As the primary designer, I led all design efforts from the ground up: building and maintaining a scalable early version of Lucy's design system, ensuring products stayed responsive and user-friendly across platforms, mentoring design interns, and working closely with product managers and engineers to keep the design process seamless and user-centered.

Outcome

From Zero to a Mature, Consistent Design System

By the time I left, the design system was mature and used consistently across every product — the foundation the team continued to build on.
