A Unified Product to Streamline Processes and Enhance Operational Performance

PROJECT MANAGER / UI/UX DESIGNER
July 2024 - Present

CLIENT: TOYOTA KALLA is an official TOYOTA dealer operating under KALLA GROUP, a prominent company based in South Sulawesi, Indonesia. TOYOTA KALLA offers various automotive services, including TOYOTA vehicle sales, after-sales services such as maintenance and repairs, and the provision of genuine TOYOTA spare parts.

PROJECT: This project was one of the ambitious initiatives from the branch leadership of TOYOTA KALLA, later escalated to KALLA GROUP. Throughout the project, I collaborated with two developers to realize their goals and address the problems they aimed to solve. The biggest challenge was adapting to the dynamic workflows across branches while developing a single product capable of accommodating seamless input-output processes and efficient data transfers. The project progressed smoothly and exceeded initial success metrics, significantly improving overall efficiency.
